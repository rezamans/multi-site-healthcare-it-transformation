## 1. Connectivity and Network

- [ ] Internet service stable and performance reasonable
- [ ] Router, switch, and access points powered and physically secure
- [ ] Network equipment and provider records current
- [ ] Wi-Fi coverage appropriate for operational areas
- [ ] Voice quality tested
- [ ] Unapproved network equipment absent

## 2. Workstations and Notebooks

- [ ] Known devices match the inventory record
- [ ] RMM and endpoint protection active
- [ ] Operating system supported and current
- [ ] Encryption verified where required
- [ ] No unexpected local administrator access
- [ ] Shared and assigned device status accurate
- [ ] Damaged, unused, or replacement devices identified

## 3. Printers, Scanners, and Peripherals

- [ ] Devices match the approved clinic standard
- [ ] Unused or duplicate equipment identified
- [ ] Label and multifunction printing tested
- [ ] Support, supply, and warranty paths known
- [ ] USB and peripheral use follows policy

## 4. Accounts and Applications

- [ ] Shared accounts minimized and documented
- [ ] Departed-user access concerns reported
- [ ] Required applications functional
- [ ] Unapproved software identified
- [ ] Group and shared-mailbox concerns recorded

## 5. Physical and Operational Controls

- [ ] Equipment protected from public access, heat, moisture, and accidental disconnection
- [ ] Power bars and cabling safe and organized
- [ ] Staff know the IT request and outage process
- [ ] Local documentation contains no visible credentials
- [ ] Manager understands open actions and ownership
