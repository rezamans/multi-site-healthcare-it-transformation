# IT Offboarding Checklist

> Generic portfolio template. HR must remain the authoritative trigger for employment status. Asset recovery remains with the designated business owner, manager, HR, or Operations—not solely with IT.

## 1. Authorized Trigger

- [ ] Written instruction received from HR or an authorized leader
- [ ] User identity and manager confirmed
- [ ] Effective date and exact access-removal time confirmed
- [ ] Immediate termination or planned departure identified
- [ ] Legal hold, investigation, or special retention instruction recorded

## 2. Identity and Access

- [ ] Primary account suspended or disabled at the authorized time
- [ ] Active sessions and application tokens revoked where supported
- [ ] MFA and recovery methods removed or transferred appropriately
- [ ] VPN, remote-access, and privileged accounts disabled
- [ ] Application access reviewed and removed
- [ ] Group, shared mailbox, and distribution access removed
- [ ] Vendor or external accounts included

## 3. Data Handling

- [ ] Manager or HR identifies required business-data transfer
- [ ] Mail and file ownership transferred only with documented approval
- [ ] Retention period recorded
- [ ] Automatic reply or forwarding approved, scoped, and time limited
- [ ] Privacy and legal restrictions reviewed

## 4. Equipment and Business Property

- [ ] Manager, HR, or Operations confirms assigned property
- [ ] Return status recorded by the responsible business function
- [ ] IT updates technical status after the device is received
- [ ] Device isolated, inspected, backed up if approved, and securely reset
- [ ] Asset marked for reuse, repair, storage, or disposal

## 5. Closure

- [ ] Access-removal time logged
- [ ] Exceptions and inaccessible systems escalated
- [ ] Manager and HR notified of technical completion
- [ ] Follow-up date scheduled for retained data or forwarding
- [ ] Request closed only after all technical actions are verified
