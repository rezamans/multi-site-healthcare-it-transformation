# Technology Procurement Workflow

## Purpose

Ensure that technology purchases have a valid business need, meet technical and security standards, and retain a complete approval and payment record.

## Responsibility Model

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Requesting manager | Defines the business need, user, location, and required date |
| IT | Defines the approved technical specification, compatibility, security, and lifecycle requirements |
| Finance | Confirms budget, payment method, tax, invoice, and accounting treatment |
| Executive or delegated approver | Approves exceptions, material spend, or new recurring commitments |
| Vendor | Supplies a valid quote, terms, warranty, and delivery commitment |

## Workflow

1. **Request:** Manager submits the purpose, quantity, location, user, and required date.
2. **Reuse check:** IT and Operations check available inventory before buying.
3. **Technical review:** IT selects or validates the specification and support requirements.
4. **Security and privacy review:** New software, cloud services, or connected devices are reviewed for data and access risk.
5. **Commercial review:** Finance validates price, payment, renewal, and cancellation terms.
6. **Approval:** The authorized approver records the decision.
7. **Purchase:** Finance or the authorized purchaser completes the transaction.
8. **Receipt and deployment:** Delivery is confirmed, the asset or subscription record is updated, and IT configures the service.
9. **Handover:** The manager or Operations confirms assignment and responsibility.
10. **Review:** Recurring services receive an owner and renewal-review date.

## Minimum Request Fields

- Business purpose
- Requester and approving manager
- User, department, and location
- Quantity and required date
- One-time and recurring cost
- Technical specification or service tier
- Data classification and integrations
- Vendor, warranty, and support terms
- Renewal and cancellation date
- Exception justification, if applicable

## Control Rules

- IT approval confirms technical suitability; it does not replace financial approval.
- Finance approval confirms funding and payment; it does not replace technical review.
- No recurring subscription should be purchased without an owner and review date.
- Exceptions must state the operational need, risk, approver, and expiry date.
