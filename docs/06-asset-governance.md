# 06 — Asset and Service Governance

## Objective

Turn inventory and purchasing data into clear ownership, lifecycle decisions, and cost controls.

## Asset Data Model

The inventory model captured decision-relevant fields such as:

- Asset type and platform
- Location and operational use
- Assignment type: staff, practitioner, shared, or loaner
- Serial number and lifecycle status
- Encryption and endpoint-management status
- Manager acknowledgement
- Replacement, repair, or redeployment decision

## Responsibility Model

| Function | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| IT | Define technical standards, configure systems, maintain technical records, and advise on lifecycle risk |
| Finance | Control payment methods, budgets, invoices, and accounting records |
| Manager or Operations | Confirm the business need, location, assignment, and return of company property |
| HR | Trigger joiner, mover, and leaver workflows |
| Vendor | Deliver the contracted service and meet documented support obligations |

IT supports the technical process but does not replace business ownership of company property, financial approval, or employment administration.

## Service and Subscription Governance

Recurring services were reviewed for:

- Active owner and business purpose
- User and device utilization
- Duplicate or overlapping capability
- Renewal date and termination requirements
- Technical dependency and migration effort
- Payment and approval record

## Outcomes

The governance model supported equipment consolidation, reuse of available devices, reduction of unused licenses, clearer purchasing decisions, and better preparation for audits and budget reviews.
