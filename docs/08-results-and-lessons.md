# 08 — Results and Lessons Learned

## Results

The transformation established a stronger operating foundation across a distributed healthcare environment:

- Central visibility for a growing endpoint fleet
- Repeatable Windows and macOS deployment practices
- Standard endpoint security and email controls
- Better asset and subscription decision data
- Reduced avoidable license and field-support costs
- Clearer ownership across IT, Finance, HR, managers, and vendors
- Documented workflows for frequent and high-risk activities

## What Worked

### Visible early wins

Solving high-friction issues early built confidence and created space for longer-term foundational work.

### Data before purchasing

A verified inventory exposed equipment that could be redeployed and subscriptions that could be reduced before new spending was approved.

### Small controlled pilots

Testing endpoint, MDM, network, and automation changes on representative devices reduced operational risk.

### Process plus technology

Tools created visibility, but clear ownership and documented approval paths made the change sustainable.

## Lessons Learned

1. A distributed environment cannot be standardized from a spreadsheet alone; physical validation and manager confirmation are both necessary.
2. Remote management must be designed around permissions, auditability, and recovery—not just convenience.
3. Vendor consolidation is valuable only when service ownership and escalation are documented.
4. Security deployment is incomplete until coverage and exceptions can be measured.
5. Automation magnifies the quality of the underlying standard, whether good or bad.
6. Small IT teams need simple controls with clear owners more than complicated frameworks.
7. Documentation is an operational control and a leadership multiplier.

## Next Maturity Steps

- Formal quarterly access and subscription reviews
- Automated compliance reporting
- Tested recovery and continuity exercises
- Configuration versioning and signed automation releases
- Defined service-level targets and trend reporting
- Annual refresh of role-based technology standards
