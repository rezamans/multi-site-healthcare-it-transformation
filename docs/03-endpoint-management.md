# 03 — Endpoint Management

## Objective

Create a sustainable way to see, support, secure, and maintain distributed Windows and macOS devices.

## Windows Management

The Windows workstream introduced:

- Central endpoint inventory and health visibility
- Remote support with administrative control and auditability
- Standard application deployment
- Operating-system and patch visibility
- Endpoint detection and response
- Role-based device and software standards
- A modular PowerShell provisioning workflow

## macOS Management

The macOS workstream used Apple Business Manager and Jamf Pro to establish:

- Organization ownership and enrollment visibility
- Standard configuration profiles
- FileVault and operating-system compliance checks
- Managed application deployment
- Administrative account controls
- Apple Silicon and Intel compatibility groups

## Rollout Strategy

1. Pilot on representative devices.
2. Validate remote support, updates, and application deployment.
3. Group devices by platform, architecture, role, and risk.
4. Roll out by location in controlled waves.
5. Track enrollment failures and exceptions separately.
6. Confirm local managers understand restart and availability requirements.

## Success Measures

- Percentage of known endpoints enrolled
- Percentage with active endpoint protection
- Percentage meeting encryption and operating-system baselines
- Remote resolution rate
- Average time to prepare a replacement workstation
- Number and age of unresolved exceptions

The goal was not simply to deploy an agent. It was to create a support model that could be measured and maintained by a small team.
